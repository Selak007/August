Welcome to August

Your private, voice-controlled browser assistant. Browse hands-free with on-device AI.

Voice control built for privacy

Unlike ordinary voice assistants that stream your audio to cloud servers, August processes voice locally right inside your browser.

🔒 100% In-Browser Whisper Whisper runs locally via WebAssembly on your CPU. Zero audio leaves your machine.
⚡ Instant Browser Control Switch tabs, zoom, scroll, mute, and search naturally without lifting your hands.
🛡 Zero Tracking or Ads No accounts, no telemetry, no tracking cookies, and no data monetization.
📖 Open Source MIT Transparent code publicly inspectable and auditable on GitHub.

Pin & Hotkeys

Quickly activate August anytime with global keyboard shortcuts or the toolbar icon.

📌
Pin August to your Chrome toolbar Click Chrome's Extensions puzzle icon (🧩) at top right, find August, and click the pin icon.
⌨️
Ctrl+Shift+Space (Mac: Cmd+Shift+Space) Open the August Side Panel and instantly start listening to your voice.
🎙
Alt+Shift+A Toggle listening on or off whenever the side panel is open.
